Subject Access Request Cards

Privacy request management for data subject rights (GDPR/CCPA)

These cards represent Subject Access Requests (SARs) - formal requests from individuals to access, modify, or delete their personal data under privacy regulations like GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection laws. Cards support request types (Data Access, Erasure, Modification), identity verification status, and sworn statement tracking.
