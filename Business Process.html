Small Card Specifications

  • Required Elements: Business Process icon, Process Name, Process ID (BPM-YYYY-XXXX), Category badge, Status badge, Duration
  • Layout: Responsive 3-column grid (1 col mobile, 2 col tablet, 3 col desktop)
  • Right Side: 2-row structure - Row 1: Category + Status badges, Row 2: Duration indicator
  • Behavior: Entire card clickable, category badge responsive (hidden on some breakpoints)
  • Excluded: No expandable sections, no participant lists, no action buttons
