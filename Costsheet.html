Costsheet Cards

Cost aggregation and budget tracking

These cards represent costsheets that aggregate expenses for cases and projects in ArkCase. Each variant shows budget summaries, cost breakdowns, and approval workflows. Status badges indicate workflow state (DRAFT, IN_APPROVAL, APPROVED, FINAL), parent Case/Complaint reference, and budget utilization metrics.
