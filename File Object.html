Security & Restriction Indicators

Visual indicators communicating access restrictions and security classifications across file objects.

Restricted Access

Limited to authorized personnel only. Requires special permissions to view or download.

Confidential

Highest level of confidentiality. Disclosure may cause serious harm to individuals or organization.

Internal Only

For internal use only. Not to be shared with external parties or the public.

PII Protected

Contains personally identifiable information. Handle according to privacy regulations.

Legal Hold

Subject to legal hold. Cannot be modified or deleted until hold is released.

Public

Public information. No restrictions on access or distribution.
